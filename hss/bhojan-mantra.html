Bhojan Mantra - भोजन मंत्र

anna grahaṇa karanese pahale vichāra maname karanā hai
kisa hetu se isa śarīra kā rakshaṇa poṣaṇa karanā hai

अन्न ग्रहण करने से पहले विचार मन मे करना है
किस हेतु से इस शरीर का रक्षण पोषण करना ह

Before we take our food, we pause to think, for what
purpose should we nurture and protect our body.

he parmeśwar ek prārthanā nitya tumhāre charṇo meṃ
laga jāye tan man dhan merā viśwa dharma kī sevā meṃ

हे परमेश्वर एक प्रार्थना नित्य तुम्हारे चरणो में
लग जाये तन मन धन मेरा विश्व धर्म की सेवा में ॥

O God, grant us this prayer, for which I bow with
respect at your feet, that my body, mind and wealth be
put to use in the service of Universal Dharma.

ॐ brahmārpaṇaṃ ̣ brahma havi: brahmāgnau brahmaṇāhutam
brahmaiva tena gantavyaṃ ̣ brahma karma samādhinā ||

ॐ ब्रह्मार्पणं ब्रह्मा हविर्ब्रह्माग्नौ ब्रह्मणाहुतं
ब्रह्मैव तेना गन्तव्यंब्रह्म कर्म समाधिना ||

The items we use to feed ourselves are Brahma. The
food itself is Brahma. The fire of hunger we feel is
Brahma. We are Brahma and the process of eating and
digesting the food is the action of Brahma. Finally, the
result we obtain is Brahma.

ॐ saha nāvavatu saha nau bhunaktu
saha vīryaṃ karavāvahai
tejasvināvadhītamastu
mā vidviṣāvahai

ॐ सह नाववतु सह नौ भुनक्तु सह वीर्यं करवावहै
तेजिस्वनावधीतमस्तु मा विद्विषा वहै ॥

Let us protect each other. Let us eat together.
Let us work together. Let us study together to be bright
and successful. Let us not hate each other.

ॐ śantiḥ śantiḥ śantiḥ

ॐ शान्तिः शान्तिः शान्तिः ||

ॐ Peace Peace Peace
